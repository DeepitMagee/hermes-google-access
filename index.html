Hermes Google Workspace

A private, single-user automation tool operated by Deepit Magee.

What this application is

This is a personal software integration, not a product. It connects Hermes, an agent application running on Mr Magee's own computers, to his own Google account. There are no other users, no accounts to sign up for, and nothing is offered to the public.

What it does with Google data

  • Gmail — reads, searches and sends email on behalf of its owner.
  • Calendar — lists, creates and edits calendar events for its owner.
  • Drive, Docs and Sheets — reads and writes files that its owner asks it to work with.
  • Contacts — reads the owner's own contacts, read-only.

All of this is confined to the owner's own Google account. No other person's data is accessed, and no data is collected from anyone else.

Privacy

See the privacy policy for how data is handled. In short: credentials are stored locally on the owner's own devices, Google user data is never shared, sold or used for advertising, and access can be revoked at any time.
